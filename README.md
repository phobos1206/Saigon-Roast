## 📌 1. Project Overview
**Saigon Roast** is a specialty Vietnamese coffee e-commerce store focusing on fresh, traceable coffee beans (robusta, arabica, blends) and brewing accessories.

* **Target Customers:** Young professionals, students, and home-brewing enthusiasts.
* **Core Problem:** Solving confusing roast levels, inconsistent pricing, and complex registration steps by offering clear product filters, guest checkout, and member pricing incentives.
